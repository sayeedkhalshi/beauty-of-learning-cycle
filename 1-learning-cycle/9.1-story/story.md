Simpler Explanation of story in human context
We are fundamentally running on flows, our context is made of flows. It's flowing in time. In general terms, flows are like habits. In medical terms, flows are like autonomous action run by an autonomous nervous system. But I think flows have much deeper significance. Conceptually a flow can be described as a story continuity in the brain's context.

A story can be in a line. Can be in a thought. Thoughts are stories and have flows and narratives, all thoughts connect like a story. If you heard - neurons fired together are wired together. Even neural connections are wired together towards a goal. They are stories. Connections of them are also stories. Breaking off a flow means breaking off the neural connections and it’s a physical thing.

Every connection in a network is a story. Story makes a story. Connects to become a new story. Stories are multi dimensional. The brain is a single story made of too many stories connecting together.

Similarly an information is formed, shaped to fit a story and flow.


This is similar to narrative identity theory by Dan P McAdams. .

The point is, the memories  a human being has, are stories and connect to become a single story, which I call main flow and many would argue it as ‘consciousness’. You are a story and you follow all the properties of a story. In the early age, you didn’t have much experience, so things out of the flow were absurd and heart breaking to you. What happens if something happens that’s totally a new story and subsidizes your own story? Can you become a new story? Of course yes but to do that you have to reshape all your stories towards that new story but not so easy. Because first you have to have all the experience required to make that new story. That is a new story in the first place simply because you don’t have any experience stored to support that thing in your system. Which means to make that yours you have to give years to make it all.
