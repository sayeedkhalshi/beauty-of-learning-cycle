Acknowledgement is actually what learning is in the most fundamental form. Before acknowledgement, it will be pre learning and after acknowledgement, it will be post learning phases of the learning cycle.

Acknowledgement have a lot of barriers. One of the barrier is desire or learning desire. Many desires can control what you acknowledge and what you reject.

# Acknowledgement with desire
When your mainflow reached a certain level of perspective, now it can access other perspectives around or in that level and below layers of it. Our desire knows where we are and it can use that level greatly even without you noticing. So your learning desires will one by one confirm that single piece of perspective you are molding in the technique phase.
