# intentionalism
Every cycle starts with a intention and ends in a non intention to actualize it. Every cycle has a lot of phases and every phase has many micro cycles. Same intentions are making the same thing. That's because everything is now-become-fragmented intention and nothing out of it. The thing, the mechanism, the actions and the ourcomes - all.

An intentional structure would be controlling the outcome of each cycle as needed. To do that, they would have to learn to figure out the micro cycles inside it. As much as they can find, the better the learner. Then they would have to engineer the changes in a way the outcome is positive. What they build is a control structure.

When all perspective in a network becomes all there is and makes it unintentional, then the fragments of it among all perspectives would trigger a desire to make all of them to be into more whole and unintentional. This process can be a continuous process.
